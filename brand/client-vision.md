# CALANTHE — Client Vision (approved)

Homepage section order, names and copy below are **client-approved
decisions, not suggestions**. Never change order or wording without an
explicit client instruction.

## Brand mood (from company profile)

- Photography: soft-focus botanicals, warm muted tones, dark green fabric,
  embossed paper. Editorial, intimate, never bright or stocky.
- Logo lockups: **Logotype** = header · **Monogram** = small uses/seals ·
  **Stacked** = formal contexts/footer.
- Emotional journey down the homepage: Hope → Confidence → Delight → Trust.

## Approved homepage flow

Hero ("Where feelings take form." · FLOWER ATELIER — UAE · [Shop Flowers]
[Build Your Own]), then exactly:

1. **NEW ARRIVALS** — eyebrow "NEW ARRIVALS" (Cinzel), Cormorant title.
   Product cards: 4:5 ClipReveal image, hover crossfade to second image
   (desktop), Cormorant name, "AED 480" Instrument, wishlist heart (44px).
   Mobile: horizontal scroll-snap row with 12% peek. Desktop: 4 columns,
   Stagger.
2. **SHOP BY OCCASION** — bento grid, 5 tiles, client's exact list:
   Birthday · Congratulations · New Baby · Love · Just Because.
   Different tile sizes, Parallax speeds 0.9/1/1.1, photo + olive overlay +
   Cinzel label. Hover: overlay deepens, letterspacing widens.
   Mobile: 2-column bento with one full-width tile.
3. **BUILD YOUR OWN** — burgundy #2E131B section (intimate rule). THE one
   pinned scrub scene: editorial photo clip-reveals → eyebrow "BESPOKE" →
   Cormorant "Made for them, by you." (SplitLines) → copy "Choose your
   budget, colours and preferences. We'll take care of the flowers." →
   ghost words Budget → Colours → Details fade through → [Create Yours]
   burnt-orange blooms. Links to /build-your-own. Mobile: pin short
   (~1.2 viewports) or unpin to sequential reveals if it feels broken.
4. **BEST SELLERS** — same grammar as New Arrivals; first tile 2x
   editorial with slow parallax and a short quote line.
5. **THE CALANTHE TOUCH** — quiet cream section: MonogramBloom divider,
   Cormorant "The Calanthe Touch", three columns "Hand-arranged daily" /
   "Thoughtful presentation" / "Delivered with care", 1–2 sage lines each,
   hairlines drawing horizontally. (Client values: quality, presentation,
   attention to detail.)
6. **A WEEKLY RITUAL** (membership) — deep-olive section; background morphs
   cream→olive on scroll enter (GSAP scrub) and back on exit. Eyebrow
   "A WEEKLY RITUAL", Cormorant "The Calanthe Membership", copy "Fresh
   flowers, thoughtfully arranged and delivered to your door every week.",
   cream-outline [Discover Membership] → /membership.
7. **INSTAGRAM** — CSS marquee, two rows opposite directions, 6 warm
   squares each, pause on hover, "@calanthe" Cinzel label. Mobile: single
   row.
8. **FOOTER** — deep olive. Stacked logo (text lockup until a transparent
   asset exists). Columns: Shop / Help (Delivery Information, FAQs,
   Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, Refund & Cancellation Policy) /
   Contact (WhatsApp, Instagram, Email). Sage text, monogram rotating
   360°/60s, "© 2026 Calanthe". All footer links resolve to real stub
   routes — nothing 404s.
